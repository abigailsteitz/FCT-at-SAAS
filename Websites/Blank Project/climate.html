Effects of Climate Change on America

Climate change affects Americans in many serious and growing ways. Rising temperatures contribute to more frequent and intense heatwaves, which can lead to health problems, especially for children, the elderly, and outdoor workers. It also increases the risk of wildfires in the West, stronger hurricanes along the Gulf and East Coasts, and heavier rainfall and flooding in many regions. These extreme weather events damage homes, businesses, and infrastructure, costing billions of dollars each year. Farmers face challenges like droughts and changing growing seasons, which affect food supply and prices. Additionally, rising sea levels threaten coastal communities with flooding and erosion. Overall, climate change impacts public health, safety, the economy, and the environment across the United States.
