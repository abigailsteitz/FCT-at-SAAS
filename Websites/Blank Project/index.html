understanding the policys

Policies in politics are the plans and decisions made by governments or political parties to solve problems, manage resources, or improve society. They are the rules or actions that guide how a government works and how it affects people's lives.

how can i make change?

To make change in politics, start by educating yourself on how the system works and staying informed about current issues and legislation. Focus on one or two causes you deeply care about, and vote in every election—not just presidential ones. Beyond voting, get involved by joining local organizations, attending town halls, contacting representatives, and using your voice online or in person to advocate for change. Even small, consistent actions can build momentum and make a real difference.
