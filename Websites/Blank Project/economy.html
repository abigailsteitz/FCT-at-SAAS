Is the Economy Doing Good?

The economy has been affected and changed by Donald Trump in several ways, both during and after his presidency. While in office, Trump implemented major tax cuts—especially for corporations and high-income individuals—which supporters say stimulated growth, but critics argue increased the national debt and widened income inequality. His administration also imposed tariffs on imports, especially from China, which aimed to protect American industries but led to trade tensions and higher costs for some U.S. businesses and consumers. Trump’s policies emphasized deregulation, especially in energy and environmental sectors, which boosted certain industries but raised concerns about long-term sustainability. Even after leaving office, Trump continues to influence economic debates, particularly around trade, taxes, and immigration. His supporters believe he champions American jobs and business, while opponents argue his approach favors the wealthy and risks long-term economic stability.
