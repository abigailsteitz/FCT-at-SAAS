Tibetan Mastiff

Home

Overview

  • Origin: Tibet and Himalayan regions
  • Breed Type: Ancient livestock guardian dog
  • Size: Very large (males 90–150 lbs or more; females slightly smaller)
  • Lifespan: Typically 10–14 years

Housing

  • Requires large outdoor space — best suited for homes with big yards or rural properties
  • Not ideal for apartment living or small confined spaces
  • Prefers cooler climates — thick double coat can cause overheating in hot weather
  • Needs secure fencing due to strong territorial instincts and potential roaming behavior

Temperament

  • Independent and strong-willed
  • Loyal and affectionate with family but can be dominant or stubborn
  • Naturally protective and territorial — excellent guard dogs
  • Often aloof or reserved with strangers

Diet

  • High-quality, balanced diet formulated for large, active breeds
  • Typically fed dry kibble designed for large breeds or raw diet depending on owner preference
