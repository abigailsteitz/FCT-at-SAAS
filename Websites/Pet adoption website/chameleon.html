Overview

  • Origin: Mostly native to Madagascar, Africa, and parts of Asia
  • Lifespan: 5–10 years depending on species and care
  • Popular breeds that are adopted are:
    1. Panther Chameleon
    2. Veieled Chameleon
    3. Jackson's Chameleon

Habitat and Housing

  • Live mostly in trees and bushes (arboreal)
  • Prefer warm, humid environments with plenty of foliage for climbing and hiding
  • Vertical enclosure with plenty of climbing branches and live or artificial plants
  • Good ventilation is critical (mesh cages preferred over glass tanks)
  • Maintain temperature: basking spot 85–95°F; ambient temp 70–80°F at night cooler (60–70°F)
  • Maintain humidity around 50–70% depending on species, with regular misting or automated misting system
  • Provide a UVB light source for 10–12 hours per day

Temperament

  • Generally solitary and shy
  • Can be stress-prone and easily frightened by sudden movements or loud noises
  • Usually not interactive or cuddly — they don’t seek human affection
  • Change colors mostly for communication, temperature regulation, or mood

Diet

  • Insectivores: primarily eat live insects (crickets, roaches, mealworms, waxworms)
  • Occasionally consume plant matter (leafy greens, fruits) but not a primary food source
  • Dust insects with calcium and vitamin supplements regularly
  • Provide fresh water by misting or using a drip system
