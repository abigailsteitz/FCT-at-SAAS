Quick Facts

  • Scientific Name: Goura victoria
  • Native Habitat: Lowland and swamp forests of northern New Guinea and nearby islands
  • Conservation Status: Near Threatened
  • Lifespan: 15–25 years

Habitat and Housing

  • Tropical rainforests and mangrove swamps
  • Prefer dense, shaded, humid environments
  • Minimum enclosure: 10 ft x 10 ft x 8 ft
  • Space to walk and explore

Temperament

  • Generally calm, gentle, and social
  • Intelligent and observant
  • Can become agitated or stressed in noisy or chaotic environments

Diet

  • Mixed grains
  • Fresh fruits (papaya, figs, grapes)
  • Protein sources (mealworms occasionally)
  • Clean water at all times
